# Feature Specification: Mach 4 Active Swarm & Infrastructure Backlog (`004-mach4-active-backlog`)

## Overview
Consolidates the completed foundation milestones (`T101`–`T105`) and the active open engineering backlog (`T106`–`T114`) across Backend, Agentic Orchestration, Frontend UX, and DevOps into standardized, persona-routed Spec-Kit tasks synchronized directly with Atlassian Jira (`PORTER`) and Google Jules (`@google/jules`).

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Completed Foundation (`T101`–`T105`)**: Tracked as `Done` in Jira `PORTER` with full correlation lineage.
2. **Open Iteration Backlog (`T106`–`T114`)**: Tracked as `To Do` in Jira `PORTER`, mapped to their respective `.jules/<persona>.md` specialist (`bolt`, `archie`, `sentinel`, `crii`, `jovol`, `palette`, `dave`, `docdoc`), and ready for 1-click preview or live dispatch from the Porter Hub GUI (`frontend/index.html` & `frontend/admin_index.html`).
